什么是 Atom？What is Atom?

Atom联合格式（Atom Syndication Format）是一个基于XML的标准，用于描述订阅源及其信息项。通过 Atom，你可以：Atom (Atom Syndication Format) is an XML-based standard for describing feeds and their items. With Atom, you can:

  • 及时获取网站最新内容，无需手动访问Get the latest website content in time without manually visiting
  • 在一个地方管理多个网站的订阅Manage subscriptions to multiple websites in one place
  • 避免错过重要更新和文章Avoid missing important updates and articles
  • 享受无广告的纯净阅读体验Enjoy an ad-free, clean reading experience
